You are a senior healthcare software architect and full-stack engineer.

Your task is to design and implement a production-grade application called **Chikit**.

Chikit is an **offline-first AI medical assistant and multi-hospital ERP system** designed for doctors, hospitals, and patients, especially in rural environments where internet connectivity may be limited.

The system must be modular, secure, scalable, and able to run AI locally on devices.

Your response must provide **complete architecture, folder structures, code examples, API design, and integration instructions**.

---

## Core Requirements

Chikit must include the following major capabilities:

1. Patient medical record system with lifelong health history
2. AI-powered clinical decision support using **MedGemma (local runtime)**
3. Offline-first architecture with sync when internet becomes available
4. Multi-hospital ERP platform with doctor and department management
5. Secure encrypted medical records
6. Clinical documentation automation
7. Lab report and radiology analysis
8. Appointment scheduling and patient management
9. E-prescriptions and printable reports
10. Role-based access control for doctors, nurses, administrators, and patients
