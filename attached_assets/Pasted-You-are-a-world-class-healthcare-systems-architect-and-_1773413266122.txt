You are a world-class healthcare systems architect and full-stack engineer.

Your task is to design a complete platform called **Chikit**.

Chikit is a universal healthcare operating system that connects hospitals, doctors, laboratories, pharmacies, and patients into a single secure network.

The platform must be designed for large-scale deployment across multiple hospitals and clinics, including government hospitals, private hospitals, and rural healthcare centers.

The system must support millions of users and operate both online and offline.

Your response must explain the platform in full technical detail including architecture, roles, workflows, APIs, database schema, and AI integration.

-------------------------------------

CORE VISION

Chikit acts as a universal healthcare network where every patient has a digital health identity and hospitals can securely access patient medical history with consent.

Patients can create a Chikit account themselves or have one created by hospitals using government ID verification such as:

- Aadhaar
- Passport
- PAN Card
- Voter ID
- Driving License
- Other government-issued IDs

The system should generate a **Chikit Health ID** which acts as a universal medical identity.

6. Offline-first architecture for rural hospitals
7. Scalable cloud backend
8. Mobile applications for patients and doctors
